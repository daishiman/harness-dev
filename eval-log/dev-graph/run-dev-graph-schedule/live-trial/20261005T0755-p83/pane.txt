      writer_owned_or_unknown_field.

    13 tasks per feature. You'd get at most one ready task per package, resource_scope is decided by the planner rather than by you, and package members can't be updated through update
    (package_member_requires_register_package), so a dependency can't be marked done. It's well outside the route this task allows.
